About

Modular design and delivery for commercial and government projects

North American Modular coordinates, oversees, and delivers custom modular structures for defense, data centers, and critical infrastructure. We hold the contract, set the specification, and stand behind every unit we deliver.

Our Model

Design coordination and oversight, in-house

Our operations and design team owns the specification, coordinates engineering with our partners, and oversees every phase of the project, from first drawing to final inspection.

Fabrication runs through a vetted network of partners, which keeps us lean without the fixed cost of an idle plant. We still set the specification, oversee fabrication, inspect the finished unit, and carry the warranty, so you have one contract and one company responsible.

It works the same way as a general contractor who doesn't pour his own concrete but is still responsible for the building.
