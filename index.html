What We Deliver

Custom modular for six core markets

Vertical 01

Data Centers & Power-Dense Computing

Prefabricated enclosures and edge structures on a predictable schedule, designed around the power, cooling, and access the load demands. Data halls and crypto/mining infrastructure alike.

  • Power, battery & generator enclosures
  • Immersion- & air-cooled mining units

Industrial & Construction
