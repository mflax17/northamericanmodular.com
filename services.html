FAQ

Common questions

Does North American Modular manufacture the units itself?

No. NAM is a design and delivery firm. Fabrication is performed by our vetted fabrication partners. NAM sets the specification, coordinates engineering, oversees and inspects the work, holds your contract, and carries the warranty.

What is your standard lead time?

Standard lead time for most projects is 8-12 weeks, depending on specifications. Your schedule is confirmed in writing with your quote, before any work starts.

How fast can I get a quote?

Quotes are typically provided within 48-72 hours of reviewing your specifications. Send drawings, a spec sheet, or a solicitation and we will respond with pricing and a delivery timeline.

Can you work from our drawings or a government solicitation?

Yes. We can price and coordinate from your drawings, performance requirements, or a formal solicitation, and we finalize every unit against the project's stamped engineering plans.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We take on single custom units as well as multi-unit and phased programs, with the same specification and inspection process for each.

What warranty do you provide?

Every structure carries a 5-year structural warranty and a 1-year parts warranty, held by North American Modular rather than passed through to a subcontractor.

Where do you deliver?

We are based in Austin, Texas and coordinate delivery to project sites across the United States.

Have a spec? We'll quote it.

Send your requirements, drawings, or solicitation. We'll respond with specifications, pricing, and a realistic delivery timeline.
