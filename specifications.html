Baseline Specification

What goes into a North American Modular enclosure

Heavy-duty materials are part of our standard specification. Final specifications are confirmed in writing with your quote.

01

Structure & Floor Frame

Welded structural steel that carries equipment loads and anchors to the foundation.

Perimeter frame
C12 × 20.7 steel channel
Cross members
C10 × 15.3 steel channel
Secondary framing
HSS 3 × 3 × ¼ and 2 × 3 × ¼ steel tube
Door openings
HSS 2 × 6 × ¼ steel tube beams and posts
Roof trusses
7-gauge structural steel (ASTM A1011)
Coating
All steel primed and anti-corrosion coated

02

Walls, Roof & Insulation

A corrosion-resistant aluminum envelope over the steel frame.

Exterior walls
0.125" 3003-H14 aluminum interlocking panels
Interior walls
¼"-½" 3003-H14 aluminum
Roof
0.125" 3003-H14 aluminum, sloped to drain
Ceiling
0.080" 3003-H14 aluminum
Base rail
6063-T52 aluminum with neoprene weatherstrip
Insulation
Closed-cell spray foam, walls and roof

03

Fasteners & Anchorage

Connections specified to the same standard as the frame.

Panel screws
¼-14 Grade 5 structural self-drilling (SAE J429), 1,000-hr salt-spray finish
Spacing
10" on center maximum at every panel seam
Structural bolts
¾-10 Grade 5 with fender washers
Anchors
Hilti Kwik Bolt TZ2, ⅝" carbon steel, ICC-ES ESR-4266
Anchor plates
12" × 12" × ⅝" steel

04

Doors & Hardware

Commercial steel doors set in structural steel openings.

Doors
1¾" flush steel commercial, outswing
Sizes
36" or 48" wide, up to 96" high
Fire rating
Rated assemblies where specified
Hardware
Heavy-duty commercial levers, cylindrical chassis

05

HVAC

Wall-mount Bard cooling with electric heat, sized for equipment rooms.

System
Bard 5-ton wall-mount air conditioner
Model
W60AF-B10
Power
208V, 3-phase
Heat
15 kW electric heat strip
