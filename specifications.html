Baseline Specification

What goes into a North American Modular enclosure

We specify heavy-duty materials as the starting point, not an upgrade. Final specifications for each project are confirmed in writing with your quote.

01

Structure & Floor Frame

A welded structural steel base that carries equipment loads and ties down to the foundation.

Perimeter frameC12×20.7 structural steel channel
Floor cross membersC10×15.3 structural steel channel
Secondary framingHSS 3×3×¼ and HSS 2×3×¼ steel tube
Door framingHSS 2×6×¼ steel tube beams and posts at every opening
Roof trusses7-gauge (0.179") structural steel, A1011
Wall caps¼" structural steel, A1011
WeldsEngineered and reviewed connections throughout
Corrosion protectionAll steel members primed and anti-corrosion coated

02

Envelope: Walls & Roof

An all-aluminum skin that won't rust, over a steel frame.

Exterior wall panels0.125" 3003-H14 aluminum, formed interlocking panels
Interior wall panels¼" to ½" 3003-H14 aluminum, per enclosure size
Roof panels0.125" 3003-H14 aluminum, formed and sloped to drain
Ceiling panels0.080" 3003-H14 aluminum
Base rail3×3×¼ 6063-T52 aluminum, sealed with ¼" neoprene weatherstrip
InsulationClosed-cell spray foam in walls and roof

03

Fasteners & Anchorage

Connections specified to the same standard as the frame.

Panel fasteners¼-14 SAE J429 Grade 5 structural self-drilling screws (ASTM A449), made in USA, 1,000-hour salt-spray finish, 10" on center max
Structural bolts¾-10 Grade 5 bolts with fender washers along the base rail; ¼-20 bolts with lock nuts at roof and ceiling
Foundation anchorsHilti Kwik Bolt TZ2 carbon steel expansion anchors, ⅝" diameter, 4½" embedment (ICC-ES ESR-4266)
Anchor plates12" × 12" × ⅝" steel plates

04

Doors & Hardware

Commercial steel doors framed in structural tube.

Doors1¾" flush steel commercial doors, outswing, 36" or 48" wide, up to 96" high
Fire ratingFire-rated door assemblies where specified
HardwareHeavy-duty commercial entry levers, cylindrical chassis
FramesKnock-down steel frames set in HSS 2×6×¼ structural openings

Electrical enclosures for a municipal stormwater pumping station

Two custom enclosures, 33 ft and 18 ft, housing electrical equipment for a flood-mitigation pumping station in the Northeast. Designed as essential public infrastructure and fully engineered, with stamped calculations covering the frame, envelope, connections, and anchorage.

Building code2021 International Building Code
Steel designAISC 360-16 and AISC 341-16
Aluminum designAluminum Design Manual (AA ADM-20)
LoadsASCE/SEI 7-16
Risk categoryIII (essential public infrastructure)
Wind124 mph basic wind speed, Exposure C
Snow30 psf roof
Floor live load250 psf, plus equipment loads
